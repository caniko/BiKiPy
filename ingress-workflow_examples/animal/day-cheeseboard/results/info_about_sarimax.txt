The SARIMAX, or the Seasonal Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average Exogenous model, is used to remove outliers from tracking, and also smoothen rapid changes in the speed of the tracked points.

Compared to ARIMA, this method introduces seasonality into the modeling, which allows us to predict movement trajectories that are recognisent to patterns throughout the entire trial.

The method has previously been used in DeepLabCut, which is the state of the art in markerless pose estimation.
