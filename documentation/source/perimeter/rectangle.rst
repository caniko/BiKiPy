======================
Rectangular perimeters
======================
These classes of perimeters can either be rectangles or squares (rectangles that have two different length of sizes).

.. note::
   Parallelograms that don't have side angles of ~90 degrees (rectangles) aren't supported.

Square
------
In square enclosures, the center and periphery are also defined as squares. The periphery is a square area that excludes the center area.

Confinement ratio of these areas can signify fear. A properly defined perimeter can cover the areas of the trial enclosure that is safer with respect to line of sight. Line of sight is often minimized during fear to decrease the probability of being spotted by a predator. The center is the area with a maximum line of sight.

The ratio is computed by dividing the seconds confined on center by periphery:

.. math::
   r_{c/p} = \frac{t_c}{t_p}

Where :math:`t_c` is the time spent on center, and :math:`t_p` is the time spent on the periphery
